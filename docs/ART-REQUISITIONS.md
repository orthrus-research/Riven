# Art requisitions

Art Riven needs and does not paint itself comes from Riven Resources, a separate art project
(16 × 16 native textures, `riven` namespace, the library's review standard). From Requisition 08
on, every set is drawn in Survey Plate ([ART-DIRECTION.md](ART-DIRECTION.md)) and passes its
acceptance tests. Riven imports finished sets unchanged (`tools/content/`), registers the blocks
and places them. Each request names what a set holds and what it is for; open requests stay here
until a set arrives.

## Open

Requisition 07: gems, giant crystals and the rare things a deep world should hold. Riven's ores
include 25 gem minerals (23 placed by its deposits, grossular and spessartine defined), from diamond
in kimberlite pipes and eclogite to sapphire and garnet in gem schist, ruby in redstone veins,
lapis in skarns, olivine in dunite, and amethyst and opal in garnet skarns. Every one shows the
painted speck overlay tinted one color, as pyrite does, and Requisition 06's habit stencils will
still tint one gray drawing per habit. Riven's geodes are lined with vanilla diorite, granite and
red sandstone, its formations are never wider than a block, and its caves grow Biomes O' Plenty's
toadstools, glowshrooms and Miner's Delight. These sets give the gems faces of their own, add the
gems a full geology holds that Riven does not place yet, and supply the large formations, rare
rocks and cave life that make the descent worth taking.

Received art serves where it fits and is not requested again: Requisition 04's quartz crystal
clusters, halite cubes, salt needles, selenite beams, geode shells with their quartz, agate and
celestine linings, gypsum flowers, helictites, popcorn, moonmilk, rimstone, cave pearls, fumarole
sulfur and obsidian shards; Requisition 05's garnet clusters, olivine crystals, jade needles, jasper
bands, moonstone seams and chromite pods; Requisition 06's habit stencils (they stay for the metal
and earthy ores), gossan, staining, dendrites and pointed formations, and its native copper.

Riven would use them in order: 31 to 36 with the gem work, which retires the tinted overlay on
every gem and gives the geodes and pegmatites something to hold; 40 and 41 as soon as they arrive,
since they retire Biomes O' Plenty's cave life and the last painted overlay; 37 to 39 with the cave
formation work; 42 to 44 with the wonders and the surface; 45 when Riven builds fluids the distant
preview can show.

Format as in Requisitions 05 and 06 (native 16 × 16 faces with side, top and bottom where they
differ, a main face and an alternate, gapless 16 × 16-tile wall reviews, overlays as transparent
cutout faces), with these additions:

- Gem ores are final ore identities drawn in their own colors, not neutral stencils for tinting.
  With GTCEu loaded a gem drops GTCEu's ore, but this face is how the gem looks in the rock either
  way. Draw the habit the gem takes in the rock its deposit names, and review each over those hosts
  as Riven shows them today (the schist unit as mica schist, basalt as leucobasalt, dolerite as
  andesite, dolostone as limestone, ironstone as quartzite) and over Requisition 04's own dolerite,
  dolostone and ironstone faces.
- Raw stones, not jewellery: crystals as they grow, often frosted, etched or dull, with light on one
  or two faces. No brilliant cuts or polished cabochons. Requisition 06's review sheets showed dark
  rims outweighing sparse grains and one stain stroke repeating per block; avoid both.
- Gems and minerals give no light; the only light here is request 40's fungi. Where a stone's color
  moves with the angle (precious opal, labradorite, sunstone), four frames of a slow shimmer are
  welcome.
- Growths are decoration subjects in four forms rising in size, from a few scattered points to a
  full cluster, on crossed planes with a transparent background. Standing pieces meet the bottom
  row and hanging pieces the top row; draw the hanging forms rather than flipping the standing ones.
  Each growth has an inventory icon of a rough specimen. Gems that grow massive rather than as
  crystals (lapis, turquoise, chrysocolla, jade, tiger's eye, amber, jet) take four forms of
  nodules and broken pieces instead.
- Pieces that build one object (a crystal's shaft and point, run pieces, column skins) join edge to
  edge; state each join and the rotations allowed, and keep axes. Clear crystals get an opaque face
  that reads as crystal (inner zoning, phantoms, a lighter edge) and a translucent alternate; Riven
  chooses after testing the distant preview.
- Calibrate before scaling: diamond in kimberlite, emerald in mica schist, the amethyst geode
  lining with its growth, the rock crystal giant's shaft and point, and the malachite block. The
  rock and mineral study leaves garnet's compact faces unresolved; settle them in calibration,
  since nine garnets depend on them.
- Record each gem's curated reference and what was taken from it.

### 31. Gem ores Riven places

One overlay per gem, main and alternate, grouped by the deposit that places it (hosts in brackets):

- Kimberlite pipes (kimberlite): diamond, sparse small octahedra and rounded dodecahedra,
  colorless to pale yellow, greasy to adamantine.
- Eclogite pods (gabbro, gneiss): pyrope, rounded blood-red grains in dark kelyphite rims;
  almandine; green sapphire; diamond.
- Garnet gem schist (mica schist, gneiss): almandine, red-brown dodecahedra wrapped by the
  foliation; pyrope; sapphire, blue barrel-shaped crystals zoned paler at the ends; green sapphire.
- Garnet skarn (limestone and dolostone at granite and dolerite contacts, mica schist): red garnet
  and yellow garnet, GTCEu's mixed skarn garnets, as granular masses with euhedral crystals in pale
  calc-silicate; amethyst, purple quartz points lining small vugs; opal, white common opal with
  flashes of precious opal.
- Lapis skarn (limestone and dolostone at granite): lapis, deep blue rock flecked with gold pyrite
  and streaked with white calcite; lazurite, rare blue dodecahedra; sodalite, royal blue with white
  veins.
- Epithermal redstone veins (leucobasalt, mica schist, gneiss, granite, andesite, limestone,
  gabbro): ruby, flat hexagonal tablets, purple-red, in the vein fill.
- Dunite lenses (gabbro, anorthosite): olivine, sugary bottle-green masses with gemmy peridot.
- Igneous apatite (gabbro, anorthosite, granite): apatite, chlorapatite, fluorapatite and
  hydroxyapatite as hexagonal prisms and grains in sea blue, yellow-green, blue-green and white;
  one structure in four colors counts as one drawing.
- Fluorite veins (limestone, dolostone, granite): fluorite, cubes and cleavage masses zoned purple,
  green and yellow.
- Mica pegmatite (granite, gneiss): kyanite, blue blades darker along their cores; pollucite,
  colorless glassy masses.
- Lithium pegmatite (granite, gneiss): spodumene, pale gray-green laths turning pink kunzite where
  gemmy; lepidolite, lilac mica books.
- Defined, not yet placed: grossular as honey-orange hessonite and green tsavorite (in skarn and
  graphite schist), spessartine as orange trapezohedra (pegmatite, manganese-rich rock).

### 33. Gem growths

### 34. Placers, float and indicators

- Gem gravel: a deposit block of rounded river gravel and sand with water-worn gem pebbles, for
  placers below gem-bearing ground; and the pebbles as ground decals in four forms for sapphire,
  ruby, spinel, zircon, garnet, topaz, chrysoberyl and diamond, on river bars and beaches
  downstream.
- Black sand (magnetite, ilmenite, zircon and monazite in dark streaks) and garnet sand

- Float: weathered fragments of an ore body scattered downslope of its outcrop, as decoration

- Kimberlite indicators: pyrope, chrome diopside and black ilmenite grains in stream gravel, a decal

### 36. Geodes, pockets and nodules

Riven's geodes (the Molten Root's gabbro, anorthosite, basalt and dolerite, radius 5 to 12) wait
for Requisition 04's shells and linings. These add linings, and the cavities the gems grow in.
Each lining block shows the crystal surface facing the void and pairs with its growth from request

- Geode linings: amethyst on a white chalcedony skin (for geodes in the flood basalts too, up to
  cathedral size), citrine, smoky quartz, dogtooth calcite, fluorite cubes, barite blades, pyrite
  druse and blue botryoidal chalcedony.
- Pegmatite pockets: a pocket wall of coarse feldspar and quartz with crystal stubs and platy
  albite, and red-brown pocket clay as a floor block, where Riven's lithium and mica pegmatites
  would open; request 32's tourmaline, beryl, topaz and kunzite grow there with smoky quartz.
- Alpine clefts: a joint wall in gneiss dusted with green chlorite, holding rock crystal and smoky

- Nodules: thundereggs in Requisition 05's welded tuff (a rough rind and a broken face showing the
  agate star), septarian concretions in shale (gray, with yellow calcite-filled cracks on the broken
  face), flint nodules in limestone (an overlay at two coverages), and giant concretion boulders
  for shores (gray, round and cracked; side and top).

### 37. Giant crystals

Single crystals many blocks long, for crystal caves, pegmatite cores and the largest geodes.
Riven would build each from pieces aligned like a log: the shaft (side and its cross-section end),
the faces of a point stepped in three pieces (side and top each), a base where the crystal grows
from rock, and a broken end. Shaft sides tile along and across the axis, so thicker crystals use the
same pieces.

- Rock crystal, clear with phantoms and striations across the prism faces, and smoky quartz,
  brown-black, for alpine clefts and pegmatite cores.
- Selenite: the point, base and broken end for Requisition 04's beams.
- Beryl, pale green-blue and opaque, and spodumene, gray-white ridged laths as long as trees, for
  pegmatite cores; microcline in pink and in amazonite blue-green, blocky with perthite streaks;
  muscovite books, stacked silvery sheets on the side and a six-sided plate on top.
- Calcite, as clear Iceland spar rhombs and dogtooth scalenohedra, for the Karst Galleries.
- Fluorite cubes (a face, an edge and a corner piece), zoned purple and green, for fluorite veins;
  halite cubes with hopper-stepped faces, white to pale pink, for the Salt Halls.

- Shields: a disc standing out from a wall (face and edge), with draperies hanging below.
- Giant stalagmite and column skins for formations two to five blocks across: fluted flowstone

### 40. Riven's own cave life

- Two cave fungi for litter and damp floors, in place of the toadstool and portobello: a pale,

- Small clustered mushrooms glowing faint green, in place of the glowshroom (light 4; Riven sets

### 41. Unstable stone

Riven's unstable floors still show a painted crack overlay. Drawn cracks in four stages, hairline,
crazed, open and about to fall, for Riven to show as a floor gives way. Each is an overlay that
reads on all nineteen hosts unstable stone covers (vanilla's six and Riven's thirteen) and leaves
their faces readable; a crack leaves each edge where it enters the opposite one, so a floor reads
as one crazed surface.

### 42. Meteorites and impact craters

- Stony meteorite: gray with round chondrules and metal flecks under a black crust; pallasite,

- Impact rocks: suevite breccia (glass and rock fragments in a gray matrix), impact melt rock, and

- Impact glass as decoration subjects in four forms: black tektites (buttons, dumbbells,

### 44. A hydrothermal salt field

Riven has rifts, magma chambers, evaporites and volcanic sulfur, but nothing where they meet at
the surface. Where a rift floor lies low over evaporites with heat below, a field like Dallol's:

- Chimneys and terraces of salt and sulfur in the colors of their age: white and fresh yellow,

- Salt pillars and mushroom-capped salt towers (side, cap top), and pool rims of brown iron-oxide
  salt.
- Salt eggs: hollow salt blisters, four forms.
- The acid brine of its pools is in request 45.

### 45. Mineral waters

Riven's water is vanilla water. A few mineral fluids, each as still and flowing faces with
animation frames and a crusted rim:

- Acid brine, green-yellow, for the salt field's pools.
- Boiling mud, gray and bubbling, for mud pots in Requisition 04's geothermal ground.
- Bitumen, black tar seeping where oil-bearing beds crop out, with GTCEu's oil deposits.
- Heavy brine, clear and dense, for pools in the Salt Halls, with floating halite rafts as a
  decoration subject.

Requisition 08: Survey Plate, the whole game in one language. Riven's blocks come from seven
productions drawn as careful naturalism, and every vanilla block it places or players build with
is vanilla's. This requisition redraws all of it in Survey Plate
([ART-DIRECTION.md](ART-DIRECTION.md)): the 565 vanilla textures listed in
[`art/vanilla-blocks.json`](art/vanilla-blocks.json) as a separate resource pack, Riven Survey
Plate, and the 3,693 resources received from Riven Resources (Decoration Library 01 576,
Requisitions 02 160, 03 176, 04 566, 05 264, 06 193 and 07 1,758). Requisition 07's subjects
stand as requested; where its format notes and ART-DIRECTION.md differ, ART-DIRECTION.md wins.
Items, mobs, the GUI and the sky stay vanilla.

Riven Resources gates this production itself, by the calibration and acceptance tests in
ART-DIRECTION.md; its style guide and worker briefs follow ART-DIRECTION.md from here on. Riven
would use the sets in order: request 46 first; then 47 and 56 for what Riven already places (the
45 vanilla textures flagged `placedByRiven`, and the imported host stones, deposits, realm rocks,
woods and ground cover), so a world reads as one language early; then 48 to 55 and the rest of
56; request 57's maps arrive with the textures they belong to.

Format: native 16 × 16 faces in the palette of `art/survey-plate-palette.json`, each with the
test kind its inventory entry or source set names; a main face and an alternate for every block
face that tiles; vanilla's sizes, frame counts, frame timing and UV layouts kept exactly for
vanilla paths (block entity sheets, the 32-wide flowing fluids, animated strips); received sets'
resource IDs, forms, faces, joins, alternates and frames kept exactly, so Riven reimports by
replacing bytes. Every native texture passes `tools/content/survey_plate.py check`, alternates
with `--alternate`, ore and gem overlays with `--host` over each host the set names.
