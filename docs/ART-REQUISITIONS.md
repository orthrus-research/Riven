# Art requisitions

Art Riven needs and does not paint itself comes from Riven Resources, a separate art project
(16 × 16 native textures, `riven` namespace, the library's review standard). Riven imports finished
sets unchanged (`tools/content/`), registers the blocks and places them. Each request names what a
set holds and what it is for; open requests stay here until a set arrives.

## Received

- Decoration Library 01 (`decoration-art/production-01`): 16 tree sets (oak, beech, birch, alder,
  aspen, maple, rowan, hazel, willow, cypress, spruce, fir, pine, cedar, mangrove, baobab; bark, end
  grain, two foliage textures, sapling, litter each), 112 decoration subjects in four forms and 8
  natural props. Tree sets imported as Riven woods.
- Art Requisition 02 (`decoration-art/production-02`), answering the first four requests (tropical
  trees, dryland trees, planks, tropical decoration): 8 tropical and 4 dryland tree sets (kapok,
  mahogany, strangler fig, rubber tree, tree fern, cacao, coconut palm, date palm, umbrella acacia,
  mesquite, olive, juniper), planks for all 28 tree sets, and 60
  tropical decorations (four liana runs with top, middle and end; bromeliads and orchids for
  branches; elephant ear and other floor plants; tropical litter). Tree sets and planks imported as
  Riven woods; the decorations wait for the forest-floor work.
- Art Requisition 03 (`decoration-art/production-03`, 176 textures): tundra, polar and alpine
  ground (cotton grass, Arctic willow, saxifrage, Arctic poppy, mountain avens, crowberry,
  bearberry, moss campion, frost-heaved stones), deserts and dry scrub (barrel cactus, agave, yucca,
  ocotillo, desert marigold, brittlebush, saltbush, creosote, tumbleweed, varnish pebbles), beaches
  and dunes (marram grass, sea oats, beach pea, sea rocket, thrift, sea holly, sea kale, strandline
  wrack), seven two-block-tall plants as joined bottom and top pieces, and a picked form of each
  berry shrub. Imported with the ground cover work.
- Art Requisition 04 (`decoration-art/production-04`, 566 textures): every request from 10 to 20,
  among them 12 signature tree sets (coast redwood and giant sequoia with root flare and burl,
  monkey puzzle, bristlecone pine, larch, dragon blood tree, Joshua tree, quiver tree, ghost gum,
  banyan, wild cherry, cork oak) and bamboo, 18 climate foliage variants, krummholz, deadwood and
  charred wood, giant fungi, the underground realms' life and minerals with six animated subjects,
  geothermal, glacial, soil, rock, reef, riverbank and wonder faces. Imported as each system that
  places it is built.
- Art Requisition 05 (`decoration-art/production-05`, 264 textures): every request from 21 to 25.
  Nineteen host-rock overlay families in four rising coverages (root threads, humus stain, rime,
  frost-shatter, dust film, gypsum efflorescence, algal biofilm, manganese waterline, silt film,
  salt bloom, dissolution scallops, calcite skin, iron-oxide weeps, damp seeps, quartz-calcite lace,
  soot, sulfur stain, glowing fissures with a four-frame pulse, ash dusting); 34 wall and floor
  blocks for the depth biomes (rooted loam to welded ash tuff and animated magma-veined rock); the
  Beneath's granulite, migmatite, eclogite, peridotite, serpentinite and blueschist with garnet,
  chromite and jadeite variants, moonstone seams at two strengths, olivine sand, serpentine gravel
  and three crystal growths; the Infernal Deep's infernal rock, slag, brimstone, cinder rock, ash
  shale, jasper bands, animated molten seams, soul ash, ash bed, ember gravel, sulfur sand and
  three growths; and the three boundary rocks. Each rock has a main face and an alternate. The
  deep realms' 19 rocks and the crust-floor and Infernal boundary rocks are imported
  (`riven-resources/requisition-05`, v98); the rest as the realm identity work places them.
- Art Requisition 06 (`decoration-art/production-06`, 193 textures): every request from 26 to 30.
  Main and alternate faces for the thirteen host stones (side and bedding-plane top for the eight
  layered ones) and alternates for Requisition 04's dolerite, dolostone, banded ironstone and
  evaporite; twelve deposit blocks (lignite, coal and anthracite seams, carbonate and non-marine
  evaporites, bauxite, caliche, phosphorite, volcanic sulfur, native copper, platinum placer, gold
  alluvium); eight ore habit stencils (disseminated, veinlets and stockwork, massive, bands,
  nodular, euhedral, books and laths, native metal) in dull and metallic lustre, with 16 highlight
  masks that need a second, untinted overlay pass; a gossan block, and copper staining and
  manganese dendrites at four coverages; and pointed calcite, ice, salt, basalt and obsidian in five
  thicknesses each way, with an inventory icon. The host stones and deposits are imported
  (`riven-resources/requisition-06`); the rest wait for the work that places them. In game,
  quartzite reads almost as limestone (a faint pink was asked for). On review sheets each stain
  repeats one stroke per block, and in the sparse ore habits the dark rims outweigh the grains;
  those are to be judged in game when they are imported.
