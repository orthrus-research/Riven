# Art requisitions

Art Riven needs and does not paint itself comes from Riven Resources, a separate art project
(16 × 16 native textures, `riven` namespace, the library's review standard), fabricated by its
Asset Factory. From Requisition 08 on, every set is drawn in Survey Plate (Riven Resources'
`docs/ART-DIRECTION.md`) and passes its acceptance tests. Riven registers the blocks and places
them; the sets imported so far sit under `tools/content/riven-resources`, and later sets arrive
by Riven's art pack, not this repository (DECISIONS.md, 2026-11-01). Each request names what a
set holds and what it is for; open requests stay here until a set arrives.

## Open

Requisition 08: Survey Plate, the whole game in one language. Riven's blocks come from seven
productions drawn as careful naturalism, and every vanilla block it places or players build with
is vanilla's. This requisition redraws all of it in Survey Plate
(Riven Resources' `docs/ART-DIRECTION.md`): the 565 vanilla textures listed in its
`docs/art/vanilla-blocks.json` as a separate resource pack, Riven Survey
Plate, and the 3,693 resources received from Riven Resources (Decoration Library 01 576,
Requisitions 02 160, 03 176, 04 566, 05 264, 06 193 and 07 1,758). Requisition 07's subjects
stand as requested; where its format notes and ART-DIRECTION.md differ, ART-DIRECTION.md wins.
Items, mobs, the GUI and the sky stay vanilla.

Riven Resources gates this production itself, by the calibration and acceptance tests in
ART-DIRECTION.md; its style guide and worker briefs follow ART-DIRECTION.md from here on. Riven
would use the sets in order: request 46 first; then 47 and 56 for what Riven already places (the
45 vanilla textures flagged `placedByRiven`, and the imported host stones, deposits, realm rocks,
woods and ground cover), so a world reads as one language early; then 48 to 55 and the rest of
56; request 57's maps arrive with the textures they belong to.

Format: native 16 × 16 faces in the palette of Riven Resources'
`docs/art/survey-plate-palette.json`, each with the test kind its inventory entry or source set
names; a main face and an alternate for every block face that tiles; vanilla's sizes, frame
counts, frame timing and UV layouts kept exactly for vanilla paths (block entity sheets, the
32-wide flowing fluids, animated strips); received sets' resource IDs, forms, faces, joins,
alternates and frames kept exactly, so Riven reimports by replacing bytes. Every native texture
passes Riven Resources' `tools/survey_plate.py check`, alternates with `--alternate`, ore and gem
overlays with `--host` over each host the set names.

### 46. Calibration

The calibration set ART-DIRECTION.md names, drawn first and taken through every test, wall,
mixed-variant wall, contact sheet and both pinned shader packs. The production scales only when
it passes; limits move only here, recorded with their reasons.

### 47. Terrain and colormaps

The 63 terrain textures: stone and its granite, diorite and andesite (natural and polished),
cobblestone and mossy cobblestone, bedrock, obsidian, dirt, coarse dirt, podzol, grass (top,
side, snowy side, tinted overlay), mycelium, farmland, grass path, sand, red sand, gravel, clay,
the sandstones and red sandstones in all their faces, snow, ice, packed ice, frosted ice's four
stages, and terracotta with its sixteen stained colors as fired clay strata (Riven bands seven of
them in badlands). Granite, diorite and andesite join Riven's host family: Riven
shows dolerite as andesite until Requisition 04's own face is imported. The two colormaps
become the surface color script.

### 48. Wood and plants

The six vanilla woods (oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak) as logs, end grain, planks,
leaves and saplings, in the families of Riven's 40 tree sets; and the 76 plant textures:
flowers, the two-block plants, tall grass and ferns, dead bush, sugar cane, cactus, vines, lily
pads, cobwebs, both mushrooms and the mushroom blocks, every crop stage (wheat, carrots,
potatoes, beetroots, cocoa, melon and pumpkin stems), pumpkins with both faces, melons and hay.

### 49. Ores and storage

Vanilla's seven ores (coal, iron, gold, diamond, emerald, lapis, redstone) and nether quartz in
the habit language of Requisitions 06 and 07, and their seven storage blocks as built metal and
mineral blocks. GTCEu's ore stencils (one per icon set, Riven Resources study 08's inventory)
redrawn as neutral Survey Plate stencils for the pack's GTCEu paths.

### 50. Masonry and joinery

Stone bricks with their cracked, mossy and carved states, smooth stone slabs, brick, quartz in
all its faces, the three prismarines, sea lantern, bone block, sponge (dry and wet), slime,

### 51. Dyed goods

### 52. Functional, redstone and creative

### 53. The Nether and the End

Netherrack, soul sand, glowstone, magma (animated), nether brick and red nether brick, nether

Riven's deep realms read as kin; end stone, end bricks, purpur block and pillar, chorus plant and

### 54. Fluids and effects

Water still, flowing and its overlay (tinted by biome as vanilla's), lava still and flowing, both
animated as vanilla's, both fire layers, the nether portal, and the ten breaking stages, which
draw on any face and so follow the ink rule as cracks.

### 55. Block entities

The 85 block entity sheets in vanilla's layouts: chests (normal, trapped, ender and the
Christmas pair, single and double), the sixteen beds and sixteen shulker boxes with the spark,
the sign, the banner base and its 38 patterns (shapes redrawn in the ink and form rules), the
enchanting table's book, the beacon and gateway beams and the end portal's surface.

### 56. Riven's received sets

Every resource of Decoration Library 01 and Requisitions 02 to 07 redrawn in Survey Plate under
its existing resource ID, in the ramps and color script ART-DIRECTION.md assigns: trees and
woods, ground cover and decorations, signature trees and climate foliage, deadwood, the realm
overlays, walls, floors and growths, the deep realms' rocks and boundaries, the host stones,
deposits, ore habit stencils, gossans and pointed formations, and Requisition 07's gems, giant
crystals, geodes, gem rock, formations, cave life, meteorites, rare ground, salt field and
mineral waters. Riven's own painted textures (dripstone block, pointed formations, ore
overlays, unstable cracks) are retired by these sets.

### 57. Shader maps

labPBR 1.3 normal and specular maps for the 108 vanilla textures flagged `pbr` and for the same
classes in Riven's sets (gems and their growths, ores, gem rock, native metals, ice, glass,
water-polished and wet faces, fluids, every emitter), authored from height maps as
ART-DIRECTION.md specifies, with emission matching each block's light.

Requisition 09: cave decorations Riven places without art. When Biomes O' Plenty's blocks left
Riven's catalogue (2026-11-03), two of their replacements had no received set; they show painted
placeholders until these arrive. Both are drawn in Survey Plate under the same tests as
Requisition 08, in the skin's and layered deep's color script.

### 58. Stonebell

`riven:blocks/decor/stonebell_<0-3>`: four forms of a small cave flower that roots in cracks of
bare stone in living depth biomes, crossed planes like Requisition 07's cave life: one to three
thin stems, each bending over in a crook under a single pale, nodding bell. It grows in twilight
and dark, so its bells may carry a faint cool tint but give no light.

### 59. Hanging roots

`riven:blocks/decor/roots_<0-3>`: four forms of roots hanging from the ceiling of a shallow cave
under living ground, crossed planes anchored at the top edge and fading out downward, fine root
threads and a few thicker roots, in the family of Requisition 05's root threads and Requisition
04's drowned roots.
