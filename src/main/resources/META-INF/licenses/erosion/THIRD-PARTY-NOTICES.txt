Riven's ErosionFilter.java adapts the Advanced Terrain Erosion Filter and Simple
Phacelle Noise by Rune Skovbo Johansen, using the CPU reference port by Luke
Mitchell, revision 57e79cc331d17e2a45c897bea9c4c022d1fc5551.

https://blog.runevision.com/2026/03/fast-and-gorgeous-erosion-filter.html
https://blog.runevision.com/2026/01/phacelle-cheap-directional-noise.html
https://github.com/lpmitchell/AdvancedTerrainErosion

The adapted file is made available under MPL-2.0. Its source is included in
Riven's matching sources JAR. Full upstream notices and license text accompany
this notice as UPSTREAM-NOTICES.txt and MPL-2.0.txt.

Riven changes include integer seeded cell hashing, physical block-unit controls,
caller-owned scratch, three bounded octaves, a slope admission mask and a local
landform-envelope displacement budget. Neither the approximate steering vector
nor the ridge tendency is authoritative surface slope or connected hydrology.

The base terrain, plate blending and temperature selection are independently
implemented Riven code. No FT's Geology implementation or assets are used.
