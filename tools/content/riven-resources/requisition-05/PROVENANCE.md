# Riven Art Requisition 05: deep-realm rocks

Original Riven artwork produced by Riven Resources for docs/ART-REQUISITIONS.md
(`decoration-art/production-05`), imported unchanged: the rocks of requests
23 to 25, a main and an alternate face each, 42 textures and the two molten-seam animation
metadata files. The Beneath: granulite, migmatite, eclogite, peridotite, serpentinite,
blueschist, garnet-studded rock, chromite pods in peridotite, jadeite veins in serpentinite and
moonstone seams at two strengths. The Infernal Deep: infernal rock, slag stone, brimstone, cinder
rock, ash shale, jasper bands, molten seams (four frames, 12 ticks each) and soul ash. The
boundaries: leucobasalt with granulite inclusions under the crust floor and heat-cracked
eclogite above the Infernal Deep. The overlays, depth-biome blocks, floors, growths and the
basement-to-root boundary wait for the cave work that places them. Offered under NRR 1.0, the
same license as Riven (its copy matches Riven's LICENSE byte for byte).

Since Requisition 06, the Salt Halls' bedded rock salt (side and top), anhydrite and sylvite also
serve as the halide, sulfate and potash deposit blocks: eight more textures, main and alternate.
