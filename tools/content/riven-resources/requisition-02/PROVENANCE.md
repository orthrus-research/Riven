Original Riven artwork produced by Riven Resources for docs/ART-REQUISITIONS.md
(`decoration-art/production-02`), imported unchanged: the 12 new tree sets of
`blueprints/trees.json` (kapok, mahogany, strangler fig, rubber tree, tree fern, cacao, coconut
palm, date palm, umbrella acacia, mesquite, olive, juniper; bark, end grain, foliage 1 and 2,

same license as Riven (its copy matches Riven's LICENSE byte for byte).
