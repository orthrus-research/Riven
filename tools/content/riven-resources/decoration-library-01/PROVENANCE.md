# Riven Decoration Library 01: tree sets and ground decorations

Original commissioned Riven artwork from the Riven Resources project
(`decoration-art/production-01`), imported unchanged: the 16 tree sets of
`blueprints/trees.json`, six 16 x 16 textures each (bark, end grain, foliage 1 and 2, sapling,
litter), 96 files, and 109 of its decoration subjects in four forms (436 files; the eight
underwater plants and three floating pads wait for a water-plant block). Riven registers the
blocks, writes their blockstates and models (tools/content/build_content.py) and places them;
the textures are Riven's own material under the same terms as the rest of Riven (NRR-1.0). Art
Riven still needs is requested in docs/ART-REQUISITIONS.md.
