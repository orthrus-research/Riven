# Riven Art Requisition 03: ground cover

Original Riven artwork produced by Riven Resources for docs/ART-REQUISITIONS.md
(`decoration-art/production-03`), imported unchanged: requests 5 to 8 of
requisition 03, 164 files. Tundra, polar and alpine ground (cotton grass, Arctic willow, purple
saxifrage, Arctic poppy, mountain avens, crowberry, bearberry, moss campion, frost-heaved
stones), deserts and dry scrub (barrel cactus, agave, yucca, ocotillo, desert marigold,
brittlebush, saltbush, creosote, tumbleweed, desert varnish pebbles) and beaches and dunes
(marram grass, sea oats, beach pea, sea rocket, thrift, sea holly, sea kale, strandline wrack),
four forms each; and seven two-block-tall plants (tall meadow grass, tall reed, tall cattail,
giant fern, foxglove spike, banana, angelica), four bottom and top pairs each, whose bottom row 0
equals the top's row 15. The picked berry shrubs (request 9) wait for berry picking. Offered
under NRR 1.0, the same license as Riven (its copy matches Riven's LICENSE byte for byte).
