# Riven Art Requisition 06: host stones and deposits

Original Riven artwork produced by Riven Resources for docs/ART-REQUISITIONS.md
(`decoration-art/production-06`, revision `ffa8cac6d125cd3a`), imported
unchanged: the host stones of request 26 and the deposit blocks of request 27, a main and an
alternate face each, 76 textures. Layered rocks have a side and a bedding-plane top.

- Host stones: gabbro, anorthosite, leucobasalt, kimberlite and soapstone; gneiss, mica schist,
  graphite schist, phyllite, slate, quartzite, limestone and shale (side and top).
- Deposits: lignite, coal and anthracite seams (side and top); carbonate and non-marine
  evaporites (side and top); bauxite, caliche, phosphorite, volcanic sulfur, native copper,
  platinum placer and gold alluvium.

They replace the Supersymmetry textures borrowed for test builds. The halide, sulfate and potash
deposits take Requisition 05's bedded rock salt, anhydrite and sylvite (`../requisition-05`).
Requisition 04's alternates, the ore habits, the surface stains and gossan, and the pointed
formations wait for the work that places them. Offered under NRR 1.0, the same license as Riven
(its copy matches Riven's LICENSE byte for byte).
